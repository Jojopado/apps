A rigged and animated voxel knight and a rideable dragon with pixel-art textures, medieval style. Use them separately, or drop in the mounted prefab with the knight already in the saddle.

Steel plate armour, royal blue cape with gold trim and an original heraldic crest. Every part is its own skinned mesh, so you can switch it on or off:

  • Body, bare face, great helm with plume, cape
  • 4 m lance with pennant, kite shield, arming sword sheathed on the hip
  • Humanoid rig: retarget other humanoid animations onto it
  • Hand sockets for your own props

Animations: Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Attack2, Block, Hit, Victory, Death, Ride_Idle, Ride_Fly, Ride_Attack

Emerald scales, gold belly plates, ivory horns and glowing amber eyes. The saddle (with harness, reins, stirrups and saddle bags) and the steel armour are separate meshes you can hide.

Voxel fire breath included: glowing cubes that turn from yellow to red and fade into smoke, with a warm light. The effect sits in the dragon's mouth and follows the head as it sweeps. It turns on with the same Breath parameter as the animation, with no scripts.

Ground: Idle, Idle_Look, Walk, Run, Roar, Sleep
Combat: Attack_Bite, Attack_Claw, Attack_Tail, Breath_Fire, Hit, Death
Flight: TakeOff, Hover, Fly, Glide, Land

The mounted prefab is the dragon with the knight already seated on the Socket_Saddle bone, so the rider follows every dragon animation, including flight. The rider has its own small Animator Controller: Ride_Idle, Ride_Fly (bool Flying) and Ride_Attack (trigger Attack).

  • 3 prefabs: Knight, Dragon, Dragon (Mounted)
  • 2 FBX models: skinned meshes, skeleton and animation clips
  • 3 Animator Controllers (knight, dragon, mounted knight)
  • Voxel fire breath effect (particle prefab + warm light), already in the dragon's mouth
  • Textures: knight 256 px, dragon 1024 px + emission (PNG, pixel art)
  • Showcase scene and PDF documentation

  • Knight: 3,036 triangles, 7 skinned meshes, 1 material, Humanoid rig (44 bones), 12 clips
  • Dragon: 6,840 triangles, 3 skinned meshes, 1 material, Generic rig (64 bones), 17 clips
  • No LODs; 30 fps clips, horizontal movement in place (no root motion); flight clips lift the body
  • Box-mapped pixel UVs, point filtering, 1 texel = 1/32 m, forward axis +Z
  • Heights: knight 2.0 m, dragon 3.7 m standing
  • Built-in Render Pipeline; URP via Unity's Render Pipeline Converter (tested with Unity 2022.3 + URP 14)
  • Unity 2022.3 or newer

Please note

  • No scripts are included: drive the Animator parameters from your own code.
  • Unity package only (.unitypackage).
