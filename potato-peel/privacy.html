A Game About Peeling Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy covers the iOS game "A Game About Peeling", made by BluePudding.

We do not collect your personal data

The game has no account and no ads. We do not collect, store or receive your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

Data on your phone

Your progress and settings stay on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple services

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. If you are signed in to Game Center, your total peeled count is sent to Apple for the leaderboard. Apple's own privacy policy applies to both.

Game engine

The game is built with Unity, which may send anonymous technical information such as device model and OS version to Unity when the game starts. We do not receive data that identifies you.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

本政策適用於 iOS 遊戲「A Game About Peeling」，由 BluePudding 開發。

我們不收集你的個人資料

本遊戲沒有帳號，也沒有廣告。我們不會收集、儲存或接收你的姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人、裝置識別碼或使用行為。

存在你手機上的資料

遊戲進度和設定只存在你的手機裡，刪除 App 時會一起刪除。

Apple 服務

購買由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。如果你登入 Game Center，你削過的總數會傳給 Apple 用於排行榜。這兩項適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。

遊戲引擎

本遊戲使用 Unity 製作，啟動時可能會把裝置型號、系統版本等匿名技術資訊傳給 Unity。我們不會收到可以辨識你身分的資料。

兒童

我們不會有意收集任何人的資料，包括未滿 13 歲的兒童。
