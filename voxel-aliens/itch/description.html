33 rigged and animated voxel alien creatures with pixel-art textures, in 10 body types. Every creature blinks, squints when it attacks and dies with its eyes closed.

  • 33 voxel alien creature prefabs (Animator + Animator Controller + capsule collider)
  • 33 FBX models: skinned mesh, skeleton and 5 animation clips each
  • 33 Animator Controllers (parameters: Speed, Attack, Hit, Dead)
  • 33 albedo textures + 19 emission textures (PNG, pixel art)
  • Showcase scene with every creature on a pedestal
  • PDF documentation

  • Blob (4): Gloop, Blorb, Mumu, Puddlit
  • Jelly (3): Drifter, Lumajel, Squibble
  • Orb (3): Watcher, Spikeball, Buzzorb
  • Bug (5): Skitter, Beetlon, Antlix, Weaver, Tickgrub
  • Quadruped (4): Hound, Rhinox, Stalker, Hoppa
  • Biped (4): Strider, Chompa, Raptix, Peepo
  • Worm (3): Wormul, Coilsnake, Larvo
  • Flyer (3): Batling, Dustmoth, Skyray
  • Crab (2): Clackjaw, Shellbot
  • Plant (2): Shroomy, Podling

  • Idle (loop): eyelids blink
  • Move (loop, in place): walk, hop, crawl, swim or fly depending on the body type
  • Attack: wind-up, strike, recovery
  • Hit
  • Death: ends lying on the ground with the eyes closed

The eyelids are bones, so blinking and closed eyes are part of the clips and work in any engine that plays the FBX animation.

  • Triangles per creature: min 84, average 269, max 396
  • 1 skinned mesh and 1 material per creature, no LODs
  • Generic rig, 7-30 bones per creature (body + eyelid bones)
  • 165 animation clips, 30 fps, no root motion
  • Textures: 32 / 64 / 128 px PNG, point filtering
  • Heights: 0.44 m to 2.53 m, forward axis +Z
  • Built-in Render Pipeline; URP via Unity's Render Pipeline Converter (tested with Unity 2022.3 + URP 14)
  • Unity 2022.3 or newer

Please note

  • No humanoid characters are included.
  • No scripts are included: drive the Animator parameters from your own code.
  • Unity package only (.unitypackage).
