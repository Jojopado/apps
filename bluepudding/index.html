About

BluePudding is a one-person studio from Taiwan making themed 3D environment packs for Unity. Every pack is built to be dropped straight into a game, not just a screenshot: modular pieces on a clean grid, consistent pivots, colliders, LODs, separate food and prop layers you can pick up and combine, and materials for both the Built-in pipeline and URP.

The focus is cozy, everyday Japanese places — the kind of shops that simulator and management games are built around.
