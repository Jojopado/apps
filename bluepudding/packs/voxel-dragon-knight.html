This is for you if

  • You want a mounted prefab with the rider already seated
  • You need ground, combat and flight animation on one dragon
  • You want a Humanoid knight you can retarget other clips onto

Skip it if: you need a flight controller. You drive the Animator from your own code.

What you get

  • Knight, dragon and mounted dragon prefabs
  • Dragon: 17 clips from sleep to landing
  • Knight: 12 clips, Humanoid rig, separate armour pieces
  • Voxel fire breath that follows the head
  • Detachable saddle and dragon armour
