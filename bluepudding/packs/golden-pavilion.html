Golden Pavilion

One pavilion, four moods.

Looking for a finished landmark scene in two styles?

Not on the Unity Asset Store yet

This is for you if

  • You want a Kinkaku-ji style temple with a garden and a mirror pond
  • You are choosing between realistic and low poly and want to compare both
  • You need a snow version that is already built

Skip it if: your project is on the Built-in pipeline. This one is URP only.

What you get

  • 4 scenes: realistic and low poly, each in summer and snow
  • FBX files for both styles, plus separate snow-cover meshes
  • Water shader with planar mirror reflection
  • Falling-snow particles, skies and post-processing profiles
