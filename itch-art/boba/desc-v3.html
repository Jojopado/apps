For Unity 2022.3+ (.unitypackage).

Boba tea shop interior, bar equipment and drinks for Unity. Every drink is built from separate layers (cup, toppings, drink, ice, foam, lid, straw) that share one pivot, so they line up when placed on the same spot.

  • Drinks as ready-made prefabs: 23 signature drinks, 14 style variations (two-tone layers, brown sugar streaks, flat lid, coloured straws), 4 drink states (half, low, empty) and 21 plain drinks
  • Drink layers: 2 cup sizes (500 / 700 ml), 21 drink colours, 11 toppings, lemon and orange slices, cheese foam, 3 ice levels, 3 fill levels, sealing film, flat lid, dome lid, straw
  • TBT_Drink component combines the layers from the Inspector; the Drink Builder scene lets you click through every option in Play mode
  • Shop modules: walls, corner posts, floors, ceiling, storefront with roller shutter, glass door and windows, staff door, awnings, signs, lights
  • Bar and kitchen equipment: front and back bar modules with opening cabinet doors and refrigerated drawers, tea urns, sealer, fructose dispenser, hot water boiler, shaker machine, ice maker, fridges, sinks, POS register
  • 3 fictional shop brands that switch with one click; all printed graphics in English
  • Demo scenes: three street-front shops (4, 6 and 8 m wide), a 10 x 12 m walk-in shop, a drink builder, a drinks lineup and a showcase

  • Cup, toppings, drink, ice, cheese foam, lid and straw are separate pieces with one pivot at the bottom centre of the cup
  • Two-tone drinks: the lower half and the upper half take different drink colours
  • Brown sugar streaks on the inside of the cup wall; straw colour can be set per drink
  • Fill level (full, half, low, empty): ice and fruit move down with the level and stay inside the cup
  • Toppings: mini boba, boba, white pearls, coconut jelly, grass jelly, pudding, aiyu jelly, taro balls, red beans, crystal boba, passion fruit pulp

  • Modules on a 0.5 m grid at real-world scale; walls 3 m high
  • 24 prefabs with doors, lids or drawers that open (TBT_Openable)

  • Fridges, cabinets and the prep fridge are hollow inside, with shelves
  • Items that sit on equipment are separate prefabs (urns, dispensers, cup stacks, receipt printer, topping pans)

  • No people or characters
  • No gameplay systems (ordering, money, recipes); the scripts only build drinks, open parts and switch shop brands

  • 236 models, 326 prefabs, real-world scale
  • Triangles per model (LOD0): min 24, average 3,309, max 28,908; LOD1 on 102 models
  • 126 PNG textures (87 at 1024 px, 36 at 512 px, 3 at 2048 px); 207 materials
  • Built-in Render Pipeline (Standard shader) plus 2 custom unlit shaders (glass, liquid); a menu tool converts the Standard materials to URP
  • Runtime scripts (namespace BluePudding.BubbleTeaShop): TBT_Drink, TBT_DrinkLibrary, TBT_DrinkBuilder, TBT_Openable, TBT_RollShutter, TBT_ShopTheme, TBT_ShopThemeSwitcher; demo walk-around scripts TBT_Explorer, TBT_ExplorerHUD, TBT_Input, TBT_ViewPoint, TBT_Grabbable
  • No animations; colliders on every prop
  • PDF manual included; Unity 2022.3 or newer

  • Asset uses textures from Poly Haven and ambientCG under CC0 1.0 (Public Domain); the fonts Lilita One, Rubik, Caveat under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see Third-Party Notices.txt file in package for details.
