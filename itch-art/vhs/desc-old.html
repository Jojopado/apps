Walk through the stores in your browser before you buy: open doors, play a tape in the VCR, pick up any of the 120 original VHS titles.

For Unity 2022.3+ (.unitypackage).

A late 80s / early 90s neighborhood video rental store. modular shell, 120 original VHS titles, shelving in empty / half / full states, counter equipment, back-room props and decor, 3 complete layouts. Built-in RP with a URP converter. All titles, cover art and brands are original. No characters included.

  • 120 original VHS titles (10 genres), front / spine / back cover art
  • Modular store shell on a 0.5 m grid: walls, aluminium storefront, glass door, staff door, floors, drop ceiling with lights
  • Wall racks, new-release wall, gondolas, kids and bargain bins, case cubbies, counters, return cart
  • Stocked nested prefabs in Half and Full states made of individual tapes
  • CRT TVs with 5 screens, VCR, rewinder, register, member PC, drop box, popcorn machine, drinks cooler
  • Openable parts: doors, drawer, lids, cooler door, drop box door (RVR_Openable)
  • RVR_Interactor: drop-in first-person E-key interaction (aim, highlight, prompt; doors swing away from the player, the glass door opens automatically)
  • 3 complete store layouts (S / M / L) and a demo scene with a stock room
  • HD hero props (CRT TVs, VCR, rewinder, register, member PC, drop box, popcorn machine, cooler, counters, cassette, rental case, neon frames): baked normal / wear maps
  • Animated scripts: CRT picture states with flicker, tape insert / eject, rewinder spin, neon flicker, auto-cycling doors (time driven, Pose(t) for cutscenes)
  • RVR_StockBatcher: static-batches shelved tapes in full stores
  • NO CHARACTERS INCLUDED

  • Real-world scale, 0.5 m grid, every prefab faces +Z, colliders on every object, LODs on large meshes
  • Built-in Render Pipeline (Standard shader) with a one-click converter to URP (tested with URP 14)
  • GPU instancing on all materials
  • Demo scene and showcase scene
  • All scripts in one namespace (BluePudding.RetroVideoRental), no third-party dependencies
  • PDF manual included
  • Unity 2022.3 or newer

  • RetroVideoRental_v1.1.2.unitypackage - import it with Assets > Import Package > Custom Package
  • RetroVideoRental_Documentation.pdf - the manual (also inside the package)

All 3D models in this package were generated procedurally by Python scripts executed in Blender. These scripts, the printed graphics generator and the Unity scripts and editor tools were written with the assistance of an AI coding assistant (Anthropic Claude). The publisher defined the content, reviewed the models and scenes in-engine and directed revisions.

No text-to-image or text-to-3D generative models were used. Textures are generated procedurally or come from Poly Haven (CC0); fonts are under the SIL Open Font License. All brand names are fictional.
