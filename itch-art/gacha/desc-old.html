▶ LIVE 3D SHOWCASE

Walk through the shops in your browser before you buy: https://jojopado.github.io/apps/japanese-gacha-shop/ - turn a knob, open a service door, play the crane game.

For Unity 2022.3+ (.unitypackage).

A busy Japanese capsule-toy shop. The machines are built like the real ones: an empty body, the capsules inside as their own prefab and a display card you can swap, so a machine can be full, low or sold out, and the service door opens to show the coin box and the mechanism. Add crane games, a blind-box wall and a prize lottery corner, then dress the shop with zone signs, banners and floor graphics. Built-in RP with a URP converter. All shop names, series and toys are original. No characters included.

  • Capsule machines in 7 shapes: single (stackable two-high), mini rack, large 90 mm, retro globe on a stand, arched premium cabinet, slim four-bay wall tower and a giant capsule novelty machine; empty / low / full / sold-out versions
  • Every machine opens: front window door, prize flap, turning knob and a service door with coin box, gears and capsule chute inside
  • Crane games (large, compact, twin set) with gantry, claw and fingers as separate parts, joystick and drop button, light-up marquee and plush prize fill
  • Prize lottery corner: tiered prize wall (A-E and Last prize), remaining-count board, ticket box with an opening lid, tickets and counter
  • Blind-box corner: boxes in two sizes for 12 series (sealed / opened / foil bag), 12-piece display cases, wall shelf, end cap and sample case
  • Capsules in 50 / 65 / 75 / 90 mm and 6 colours: full (shell, leaflet and toy are separate), empty and open
  • 67 original mini toys in 12 original series
  • Modular shop shell on a 0.5 m grid, glass storefront with automatic sliding door, fascia, awning, window stickers, sidewalk and road
  • Shop dressing: light-box zone signs, hanging banners, ceiling rafts, floor graphics, wall panels, in-store POP, register counter with POS, change machine, display case
  • HD hero models with baked panel seams, screws, embossed lettering and light wear
  • Bonus scripts: knob turn drops a capsule, capsules open, crane plays a full round, automatic doors, sign flicker (each with Sample(time) for cutscenes)
  • Demo scene with three furnished shops (S / M / L) and a comparison area, plus a showcase scene
  • NO CHARACTERS INCLUDED

  • Real-world scale, 0.5 m grid, every prefab faces +Z, colliders on every object, LODs on large meshes
  • Built-in Render Pipeline (Standard shader) with a one-click converter to URP (tested with URP 14)
  • GPU instancing on all materials
  • All scripts in one namespace (BluePudding.JapaneseGachaShop), no third-party dependencies
  • PDF manual included
  • Unity 2022.3 or newer

  • JapaneseGachaShop_v1.2.5.unitypackage - import it with Assets > Import Package > Custom Package
  • JapaneseGachaShop_Documentation.pdf - the manual (also inside the package)
