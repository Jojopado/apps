Additional compatibility information

Works in the Built-in Render Pipeline out of the box. URP: materials are not converted automatically. Install the Universal RP package, assign a URP asset in Project Settings > Graphics, then run Tools > Conveyor Sushi Shop > Convert Materials to URP (tested with URP 14). Until the converter is run, materials show pink in URP. HDRP is not supported.

Models, prefabs and a demo scene of a Japanese conveyor-belt sushi restaurant (kaiten-zushi): dining room, kitchen, staff room, shop front, street and parking lot. The restaurant name, signs, menus and posters are fictional.

  • Building: exterior walls (plain, window, door), kitchen tile walls, kitchen wall with steel back door, partition walls (1 m, 2 m, with door, with pass-through window, with lane opening and strip curtain), interior wall with hinged door, pillar, wood / kitchen / concrete / carpet / paving / asphalt floors, curb, wooden louver ceiling, kitchen ceiling, noren, shop sign, entrance canopy, automatic door panel, entrance mat
