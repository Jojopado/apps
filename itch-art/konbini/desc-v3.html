For Unity 2022.3+ (.unitypackage). Other engines are not supported.

A Japanese convenience store (konbini) from the parking lot to the backroom: modular building pieces, equipment, 427 products, 4 store brands and 6 stocked store layouts.

  • HINATA MART (teal / yellow), MOMIJI STORE (red / orange), AOZORA MART (blue) and SUMIRE MART (purple). One click changes sign bands, logo, aisle signs, fridge headers, posters, vending machines and delivery truck. All brands are original and fictional.

  • Small, Medium, Medium Cafe, Medium Station, Large and Large Urban, as stocked prefabs
  • Demo scene: 16 x 12 m store with walk-in cooler, receiving area, backroom, restroom and parking lot
  • Showcase scene with the prefabs by category

  • Drinks: cans, bottle cans, PET bottles with see-through drinks, cartons, chilled cups
  • Alcohol: beer, chuhai, one-cup sake, sake and shochu bottles, wine, whisky
  • Snacks and noodles: chips, candy, cookies, chocolate, gum, otsumami, cup / bowl / bag noodles
  • Fresh food: onigiri (wrapped and unwrapped), bento and rice bowls with separate lids, sandwiches, bread, salads, desserts
  • Ice cream, frozen food, bagged ice, eggs, tofu, natto, yogurt
  • Daily goods, cosmetics, socks, masks, magazines, manga, prepaid cards, seasonal goods
  • Hot food: oden, fried items, steamed buns, coffee
  • Product list as an asset and as a CSV (Japanese and English name, brand, category, price, size)

  • Building: walls, storefront glass, automatic entrance, staff / restroom / steel back doors, floors, ceiling, light fittings, sprinkler, smoke detector, emergency light, air vent, sign bands
  • Shelving: wall, peg-hook and island gondolas, end cap, magazine rack, manga rack, cosmetics gondola, bakery rack, gift-card wall, price tags
  • Refrigeration: drink cooler, walk-in cooler (panels, racks, evaporator, drink cases), upright freezer, open multideck case, ice-cream chest, ice-cup and bagged-ice freezers
  • Checkout: counter modules with customer and staff side parts, POS register, receipt printer, self-checkout, tobacco rack with removable packs, back counter
  • Hot food: oden pot, fried-food case, coffee machine, steamed-bun case, microwaves, hot-drink case
  • Services: ATM, copier, trash station, parcel lockers, security cameras with monitors, fire equipment
  • Backroom: receiving area with pallet, scale, inspection table and hand truck, racks, staff desk, lockers, roll cages
  • Restroom, eat-in corner
  • Outdoor: 3 vending machines, gacha machines, ice-cream freezer, pylon sign, parking lot, bike rack, bench, smoking area, delivery truck with cargo doors and tail lift
  • Seasonal sets: New Year, Valentine, summer, Halloween, Christmas
  • Store states: discount stickers, sold-out cards, spills, broken bottle, damaged carton, footprints, overflowing trash

  • Fridge, freezer, cooler and vending machine doors, oden lid, hot cases, coffee hopper, microwave, ATM and copier covers, trash bins, cash drawer, self-checkout, parcel locker doors, desk drawers, lockers, bakery lids, toilet lid, truck doors and tail lift

  • Opening parts, automatic door, shelf stocking, product catalog, cartons, cargo loading, brand switch, season switch, day / evening / night and rain, security camera monitors

  • Unity 2022.3 or newer; Built-in Render Pipeline, with a converter to URP (tested with URP 14). HDRP is not supported
  • Real-world scale, 0.5 m grid, 3 m walls, pivots at floor level, +Z is the front
  • Models: 473 FBX files. Triangles per prefab (without the store layouts): 2 to 85,450, average about 2,400
  • Textures: 829 PNG, 256 to 8192 px (product label sheets are 8192); albedo, normal, metallic / smoothness and occlusion maps; UV mapped
  • LODs on larger models, box or mesh colliders, GPU instancing enabled
  • No rigging and no animation clips; doors and lids move by script
  • English PDF manual included
  • NO CHARACTERS INCLUDED. The demo scene has a fixed camera, no player controller
