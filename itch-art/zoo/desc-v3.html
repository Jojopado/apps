LOW POLY ZOO ENVIRONMENT

Low poly animal, building and nature models with vertex colours, plus one demo zoo scene.

There are no visitor or staff models.

  • Built-in Render Pipeline only. URP and HDRP are not supported
  • Animal prefabs are static models with no rig and no animation clips
  • The animal movement in the demo scene depends on the demo scene layout and does not work on its own in other scenes
  • Terrain, water and rock meshes in the demo scene are not modular pieces

  • Animals: lion, giraffe, zebra, elephant, penguin, polar bear, monkey, flamingo, tortoise, sheep, rabbit, meerkat

  • Buildings: entrance gate, ticket booths, gift shops, cafes, restrooms, drink stands, kiosk, barn, viewing and thatch shelters, tree house, penguin slide, cable car stations, towers and cabins
  • Trees: acacia, coral, eucalyptus, fig, jungle, palm, pine, round and spruce trees, snag, utility tree
  • Plants: bushes, ferns, grasses, aloe, bamboo, prickly pear, protea, reeds, restio, red hot poker, fireweed, tundra shrub
  • Rocks: rocks, red rocks, ice blocks, ice rocks, inukshuk, log
  • Props: bench, picnic table, lamp, fence, stone fence, railing, fountain, trash bin, info panel, sign post, ice cream cart, balloon stand, trough, hay bale, rope post

  • One demo zoo scene
