🍜 Build a cozy Japanese ramen shop – and make it playable.

Most food and restaurant packs are scenery. This kit is built for cooking and restaurant simulators: the ramen is split into stages and parts, the kitchen tools are separate grabbable props, and the shop follows a strict modular grid so you can lay out your own restaurant in minutes.

👉 Try before you buy: Japanese Ramen Shop – Free Sampler (10 free props) on my itch page.

  • Ramen in layers: 3 bowl styles sharing one inner profile, plus tare, 4 broths (shoyu, tonkotsu, miso, shio) and noodles that snap into any bowl at local (0,0,0)
  • 7 separate toppings: chashu, ajitama, nori, naruto, negi, menma, corn
  • Raw to cooked: raw noodle portions → tebo baskets → noodle boiler with a visible boiling-water tank → bowl
  • Prep: chashu roll, negi stalk, eggs, knife, cutting board, ladles, strainer, squeeze bottles, 6 prep pans (empty and filled)
  • Order flow: ticket (shokken) machine with 12 printed menu buttons, tickets, register, cash tray, coins
  • Plating counter: customer top, serving pass and a chef worktop at working height
  • Kitchen timers, fridge with hinged doors, dirty bowls, dish rack, bowl stacks

  • Walls (1 m / 2 m, window, door, kitchen tile, partition, pass-through), pillars, floors, ceiling, eaves, sidewalk
  • Counter segments, L-corner and end caps; stools
  • Noodle boiler, stoves, sink, worktables, range hoods, exhaust fan, shelves
  • 0.5 m grid, 3 m walls, every pivot at floor level – pieces just snap together

  • Noren curtain, akachochin lanterns, shop sign, menu plaques, posters, chalkboard sign, vending machine, lattice screen, plants, clock, TV, air conditioner

  • All lettering is original and lives in one editable texture atlas – rename the shop by editing a single image

  • Unity package (2022.3+): 130 prefabs, 2 demo scenes (furnished 8 x 4 m shop with day & night lighting + full catalog), PDF documentation
  • FBX + PNG textures + material table + docs – for Godot, Unreal, Blender and other engines

  • 130 prefabs (129 models + assembled fridge), real-world scale, Y-up, +Z forward
  • LOD0/LOD1 on every model over 1,500 triangles; colliders on every prefab
  • Tiling PBR materials (albedo, normal, metallic/smoothness), 2K textures, 1 UV unit = 1 m
  • Built-in Render Pipeline + one-click converter to URP (Tools > Japanese Ramen Shop), tested with URP 14
  • Most props under 5,000 triangles
