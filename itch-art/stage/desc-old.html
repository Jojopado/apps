Materials use the Built-in Standard shader. For URP, install Universal RP and run Tools > Japanese Live Stage > Convert Materials to URP once (tested with URP 14). Materials show as pink in URP until this step is done.

CENTER STAGE · MOVING HEADS · LED VIDEO · 4 SHOW THEMES · S / M / L VENUES

The lights really move, the beams change colour, effect machines fire, and every poster, banner, T-shirt and LED screen changes with the show theme.

This pack contains venues, stages and equipment only. There are no audience, performer or staff models. The demo scenes show empty venues, ready for your own characters.

  • Architecture: modular black walls (4 m high), black-painted brick, acoustic foam wall, white backstage walls, soundproof double door, staff door and emergency exits, 3 x 3 m passage, floors, ceilings with ducts
  • Stage: 2 x 2 / 1 x 2 decks with velvet skirt or LED edge, drum risers, stairs, round lift with LED ring, pop-up lift on a telescopic column, 5 m round center stage, trolley cart, banner, pipe and drape
  • Truss: 29 cm box truss 0.5-3 m, corners (2-way, 3-way, T, cross), 3 m / 5 m towers, chain hoist, clamp
  • Lighting: moving spot / wash / beam (floor and hung), LED par, profile, LED batten, blinder, strobe, laser, followspot, mirror ball
  • Audio: line array (hung arrays of 6 and 10, curved), subwoofer, point source on stand, floor wedge, wall speaker
  • Video: LED walls with 4 screen contents (or your own video / RenderTexture), switched-off LED wall, side screens, ticker, LED tile, cue monitor, broadcast cameras (tripod, pedestal, jib, dolly on track, platform, PTZ)
  • FOH: digital mixer, lighting console, FOH desk, amp rack, stage box, cable ramp, cables
  • Backline: full drum kit and every drum piece, electric / bass / acoustic guitars, amps, keyboard, synth, mics and stands, DJ booth, pedalboard, guitar rack
  • Effects: CO2 jet, flame unit, cold spark fountain, confetti cannon, fog machine, hazer
  • Venue: bar counter, back bar, drinks fridge, cups, coin lockers, ticket counter, queue stanchions, stage barrier with corner, barrier fence, mesh site fence, posters, neon signs, exit signs
  • Merch and backstage: merch table and wall, T-shirts, towels, penlights, uchiwa fans, dressing mirror with bulbs, sofa, costume rack
  • Roof: steel roof truss, H-beam, roof deck, catwalk, high-bay house lights
  • Seating: pipe chairs (single and rows of 10), stadium seats, 6-row tiered seating blocks, aisle stairs with step lights
  • Road cases S / M / L (openable M / L), cable trunk, ladder, platform truck
  • Venue states: confetti, streamers and trash for after the show; tape marks and setlist for the setup
  • 3 ready-made stages (8 x 4, 14 x 6 with truss, 20 x 8 with thrust)
  • 3 demo scenes (live house, hall, arena) and a showcase scene

All artist, venue and brand names in the kit are original and fictional.
